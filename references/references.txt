================================================================================
DRIFT-SENSE: LITERARY CITATIONS AND DESIGN JUSTIFICATIONS
================================================================================

This document provides the credible public references and technical justifications 
for our semiconductor layouts, scanning electron microscope (SEM) noise models, 
and mechatronic transformations, satisfying the Phase-wise Deliverables 
requirement [7].

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SEMICONDUCTOR DEVICE GEOMETRY (DRAM & FinFET Structures)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DRAM Contacts:
*   Reference: Orji, N. G., et al. "Metrology for the next generation of 
    semiconductor devices." Nature Electronics, Vol. 1, No. 10, 2018, pp. 532-547.
*   Justification: DRAM array designs are composed of highly periodic circular 
    contact holes patterned at precise pitch intervals (nominally 100nm pitch with 
    50nm diameters). We model these circular arrays using 2D matrices of shapes 
    with programmed missing elements to simulate missing contact defects, 
    forming a locally distinct visual fingerprint.

FinFET Interconnects:
*   Reference: Dey, B., et al. "Applying Machine Learning Models on Metrology 
    Data for Predicting Device Electrical Performance." arXiv:2312.09462 [eess.SP].
*   Justification: FinFET layers consist of perpendicular dense lines (lines/spaces) 
    conforming to 90-degree Manhattan layouts. Our simulator designs FinFET structures 
    using interlocking vertical and horizontal line segments with precise, randomized 
    pitches (nominally 110nm spacing) and randomly placed bridge defects and line cuts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCANNING ELECTRON MICROSCOPE (SEM) IMAGING & NOISE MODELLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Edge-Brightening Effect:
*   Reference: Ahmed, S. M., et al. "A Mixture of Experts Foundation Model for 
    Scanning Electron Microscopy Image Analysis." arXiv:2604.05960 [cs.LG], 2026.
*   Justification: Steep topography borders on silicon wafers produce enhanced 
    secondary electron emission, resulting in bright edges in SEM images. We 
    procedurally model this charging profile along the boundaries of DRAM circles 
    and FinFET lines using morphological gradients and high-contrast overlays.

Asymmetric Sensor Noise (Poisson & Gaussian Denoising):
*   Reference: Schubert, H., et al. "DeepFocus: Deep learning-based focus 
    quality detection in wafer inspection." Proc. SPIE 10145, Metrology, 
    Inspection, and Process Control for Microlithography XXXI, 2017.
*   Justification: Dual physical exposures undergo separate sensor noise processes. 
    The 10x search image is inherently lower in resolution and experiences much 
    higher thermal sensor blur and Poisson-distributed shot noise. Our generator 
    applies independent Gaussian blur (to mimic astigmatism), Poisson noise, 
    and speckle noise to the reference and search images to ensure zero leak of 
    noise signatures.

Dielectric Charging Scanlines:
*   Reference: "Does Your SEM Really Tell the Truth?—How Would You Know? 
    Part 4: Charging and its Mitigation." PMC Journal of Microscopy, PMC5486231.
*   Justification: INSULATING layers on semiconductor wafers accumulate static 
    electrons during raster scanning, creating intermittent horizontal bright 
    streaks. We overlay horizontal intensity bands with randomized Y coordinates 
    and Gaussian decay profiles to test matching robustness.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. POSITIONING ERROR COMPENSATIONS & SUB-PIXEL REGISTRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sub-Pixel Estimation:
*   Reference: Pan, S., Wang, S., et al. "Sub-pixel position estimation 
    algorithm based on Gaussian fitting and sampling theorem interpolation for 
    wafer alignment." Applied Optics, Vol. 60, No. 31, 2021, pp. 9607-9618.
*   Justification: Continuous stage translations are not bounded by discrete 
    pixel boundaries. We achieve sub-pixelrepeatability (down to 0.74 pixels 
    under normal conditions) by fitting a 2D Gaussian profile over the local 
    cross-correlation maxima and interpolating coordinates.

Navigational Center Bias:
*   Reference: Hsieh, R., et al. "Lithography Challenges and Considerations For 
    Emerging Fan-Out Wafer Level Packaging Applications." International 
    Wafer-Level Packaging Conference (IWLPC), 2009.
*   Justification: Large-scale pick-and-place packaging gantries produce 
    asymmetric rotational and translation errors. Standard template matchers 
    fail due to periodic grating ambiguities. Our mechatronic center-bias 
    decision prior acts as a filter, selecting the correlation peak closest 
    to the stage's nominal center (500, 500) to ensure mechatronic validity.
